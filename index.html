Hebei Charity Events is the community platform that connects people across Shijiazhuang, Baoding, Tangshan, Qinhuangdao, Handan, Langfang, Chengde and Zhangjiakou with the fundraisers that keep our province caring. Every fun run, gala dinner, silent auction, concert and community drive listed here is run by a registered Hebei charity — and every ticket you buy is a donation that stays local.

💡 Our mission

We exist to make giving easy. Too many small Hebei charities run brilliant events that nobody hears about, while thousands of residents would happily take part if they only knew. This platform closes that gap: one searchable calendar of every registered charity event in the province, with transparent fundraising progress so donors can see exactly what their money has achieved.

✓ Registered charities only ✓ Transparent fundraising goals ✓ Free to list & free to search ✓ Policy-checked events
